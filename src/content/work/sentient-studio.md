## What I worked on

Studio life — short cycles, varied clients, lots of practice across product categories. I picked up the rhythm of designing under constraint: a fixed scope, a real deadline, and a client who needed to be persuaded.

## What I am proud of

Cut turnaround time on early-stage projects by tightening the research-to-prototype loop into something I could do in days instead of weeks.

Pushed for prototypes as the deliverable, not static screens. Clients stopped arguing about details once they could click through the thing.

Earned my fundamentals here — type, hierarchy, motion. The stuff a CS degree didn't give me but that this studio did.
