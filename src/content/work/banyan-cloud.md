## What I worked on

Banyan was my first founding-designer role. I joined when there was no UI to speak of and helped shape the product strategy alongside the founders. Over two years I designed the surfaces for multiple cloud-security services — posture management, identity, runtime protection — and got each from whiteboard to shipped.

## What I am proud of

Designed and open-sourced Roots, the design system that powered the entire product. Working closely with engineering so the system was true to the code, not just a Figma artifact.

Translated dense security concepts into screens that operators could trust. Security tools fail when they overwhelm — every layout was an exercise in restraint, every default chosen carefully.

Held the line on quality at MVP pace — the kind of design hygiene most early-stage products give up on under deadline pressure.
